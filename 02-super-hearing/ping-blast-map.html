Where the jobs went: before and after 4.2

Before 4.2, every job was taken by the hero who lives in that area. After 4.2, the jobs in three areas (Uptown, Harborside, Old Town) are being taken by heroes from elsewhere, and Eastgate's jobs have moved from Meteor Mite to The Gale. The flow charts show where each area's jobs went. The ring map at the bottom shows every ping. Location still matters: every hero was pinged for the same one to three neighbouring areas before and after 4.2, and no hero was ever pinged for a new area. What changed is who was first in line inside each neighbourhood.
