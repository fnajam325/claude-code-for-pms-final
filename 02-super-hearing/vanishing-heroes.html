1. The mission

From the moment something goes wrong to the moment a phone buzzes. Click a step for the plain-English version and the file it lives in.

The trap, in four moves. The window got shorter (step 8), so more offers went unanswered. An unanswered offer cost points (step 11). The lower score was read the next time (step 4), which pushed the hero down the list (step 6). Lower on the list means fewer offers, and fewer offers means no chance to earn points back.

    2. Asked versus built

    Each change was built as written. The question is whether it did what it was meant to do.

    3. The villains

    There are no crashes. The code does what it says. The trouble is what it says.

    4. The hero health lab

    Pick a hero, change the rules, and watch their score history replay. The red line is today's rules. The blue line is yours.

    5. Orders for the developer

    In priority order. Tick them off as they land. Each one says what "done" looks like.

      Two cautions to pass on.
      • Raising the floor alone is not enough. At a floor of 0.3, neighbours within about 13 minutes' travel still outrank a hero at the floor. Orders 1, 2 and 3 matter more.
      • A 2019 note in the code worries that someone who truly stopped answering should stay down. That is a real trade-off. Flag unresponsive heroes to their handler instead of quietly demoting them.
