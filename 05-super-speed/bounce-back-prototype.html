    The partial refuser

    This hero says yes to 1 job in every 3 and misses the other 2. That is 9 offers a week, 3 taken and 6 missed, for 10 weeks.

    Please read: this model assumes a score climbs back toward the middle even in a week with no yes at all. The real routing rule does not work this way today. Whether the score should climb back, and how fast, is a design choice, not a fact about the system.

    How the math works

    Each week is treated as one batch. A yes adds 0.08. A missed offer takes away 0.12. The score stays between 0 and 1. Then, with the bounce-back rate, the score moves that fraction of the way back toward 0.5. Real weeks are not split into single offers, so this is an approximation.
