    Why change the alert

    • Hard to read. The current text is small and light grey. The proposed text is larger and darker, with the time in big numbers.
    • Same sound for everyone. Every phone goes bing, so a responder cannot tell whose offer it is. Each responder can have a sound of their own.
    • Too bright at night. The current screen is bright white. A dark theme gives a softer screen.

    Still to decide

    • Dark at night. Should the new design switch to dark by default after dark, or stay light until a handler turns dark on?
    • How loud. How loud should each sound be, and should the sound change when a phone is set to quiet?

    Rook Industries is a fictional company. The responders and incidents on this page are examples only.
