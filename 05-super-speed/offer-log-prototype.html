Why this matters

A missed offer can mean two things. The responder said no, or the timer ran out before they answered. If we count both as one "not taken," we cannot tell which problem we have. A timeout points to a timer that is too short. A no points to the offer itself, or to who got it.

What gets recorded

We would add four fields to every offer:

  • Outcome type: Yes, No, or Timed out.
  • Timestamp: the exact time the outcome was set.
  • Seconds waited: how long the offer stayed open.
  • Offer window length: the time limit used, either 60 or 90 seconds.
