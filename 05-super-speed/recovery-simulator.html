The "says no to everything" test

One hero gets 10 jobs a week and says no to all of them, for 10 weeks.

Even with a bounce back, a hero who keeps saying no stays near the bottom. A softer timeout only helps when some of the misses are timeouts.

The four heroes in trouble are our test case. Ashgrove and Halfmoon score high under every rule, so their score isn't what's dragging them down. Something else is.
